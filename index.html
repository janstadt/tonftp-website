What we do

One team across the whole product — not just the demo.

Most software problems are not just code problems. We take responsibility for the build, the release, and the day-two operation of what we ship.

Web applications

Product-grade front ends and server-rendered sites in TypeScript and React — fast, accessible, and maintainable by more than the person who wrote them.

Native iOS & Android

Real native apps — device capabilities, offline behaviour, push, and store-grade polish. React Native and Expo with native modules, plus Swift and Kotlin where the platform demands it.

  • iOS
  • Android
  • React Native

Backend & APIs

Service and data design, authentication, background jobs, and third-party integrations — built to be observable, not to be guessed at.

  • APIs

Kubernetes and declarative delivery: everything in git, reviewed like code, reconciled automatically. Sealed secrets, CI/CD, backups, and monitoring included.

How we work

Small, direct, and accountable.

You work with the people writing the code. Short feedback loops, working software early, and no surprises at the end.

Scope

We start with the outcome, not the ticket list — then agree on the smallest version worth shipping and what "done" means.

Build

Working software in front of you early and often. Reviews, previews, and progress you can click, not status reports.

Ship

Production release the boring way: automated, reversible, and measured — web deploy, App Store, or Google Play.

Operate

Monitoring, backups, and updates. We stay on the hook for what we built instead of handing it over and disappearing.

Company

TONFTP LLC is an independent software development company. We own and operate tonftp.com — the domain is the company's home on the web, and the services we publish under it.

Our focus is web and native software: applications people use on a browser and on the devices in their pockets. That means front-end and mobile engineering, the backend services behind them, and the infrastructure that keeps them online.
